# 💰 ECONOMICS

## 🎯 Goal

Evaluate whether this AI Audit Tool can generate meaningful revenue for Credex if deployed.

This is a lead-generation product, not a direct revenue product.  
So the economics depend on:

Audit → Consultation → Credit Purchase

---

## 💵 1. What is a Converted Lead Worth?

Assumption:

- Credex helps companies optimize AI/tooling costs
- A typical client spends $500–$2,000/month on AI tools
- Credex earns via:
 - consulting
 - credit partnerships
 - optimization services

### Estimated value per converted customer:

plaintext id="b5v8l2" Average monthly revenue per client = $200 Retention = 6 months  → Total value per customer = $200 × 6 = $1,200

👉 Estimated LTV = $1,200

---

## 📉 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

From GTM strategy (0$ budget), CAC is mostly time-based.

### Channel breakdown:

| Channel | Cost | Notes |
|--------|------|------|
| Reddit posts | $0 | organic |
| X (build in public) | $0 | organic |
| Cold DMs | $0 | time investment |

👉 Initial CAC ≈ $0–$10 per user (time cost)

---

## 🔁 3. Conversion Funnel Assumptions

We estimate realistic conversion rates:

plaintext id="9e8rfp" 100 users run audit ↓ 30% see meaningful savings → 30 users ↓ 15% book consultation → 4–5 users ↓ 40% convert to paying → 2 users

---

## 💰 4. Revenue from 100 Users

plaintext id="1c4l1r" 2 paying customers × $1,200 LTV = $2,400 revenue

👉 Revenue per 100 users = $2,400

---

## 📊 5. Is This Profitable?

plaintext id="4fzx8c" CAC ≈ $0–$10 LTV = $1,200  → LTV >> CAC ✅

👉 Strong positive unit economics

---

## 📈 6. What Conversion Rate Makes This Work?

Let’s define minimum viability:

plaintext id="fdzzks" Audit → Paid conversion ≥ 1%

### Example:

plaintext id="tzldu2" 100 users → 1 customer → $1,200 revenue

👉 Still profitable at low scale

---

## 🚨 7. Pivot Condition

If after real usage:

plaintext id="a5h0m2" < 5% of users book consultation

👉 Then:

- recommendations may lack trust  
- savings may feel unrealistic  
- wrong audience targeted  

→ Requires product or positioning change  

---

---

## 🧠 Key Assumptions

- Users trust audit results  
- Savings are meaningful (> $50/month)  
- Target users are cost-sensitive  
- Credex can convert consultations effectively  

---

## ⚠️ Risks

- Users may not act after seeing savings  
- API pricing complexity not fully captured  
- Tool recommendations may not fit all workflows  

---

## 💡 Insight

This tool works because:

It captures users at a high-intent moment—when they suspect they are overspending.

Even low conversion rates can produce strong returns due to high LTV.
