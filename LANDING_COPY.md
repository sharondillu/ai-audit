## FAQ

### 1. How does the audit work?
We analyze your AI tools, team size, and use cases using a rule-based system to identify overlaps, inefficiencies, and optimization opportunities.

---

### 2. Is this using AI to generate recommendations?
No — recommendations are deterministic and rule-based to ensure consistency, transparency, and reliability.

---

### 3. Do I need to connect my accounts?
No. The audit is based on the inputs you provide — no integrations or access required.

---

### 4. What happens to my data?
Your data is stored securely. Public reports exclude sensitive details like email and company name.

---

### 5. Is this really free?
Yes. The audit is free. In cases with high savings potential, we may recommend a consultation to help implement optimizations.
